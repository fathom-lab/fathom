The Paper

Fathom: Measuring Reasoning Depth

SAE circuit attribution reveals structurally different computational signatures for surface recall vs explanatory reasoning. The depth ratio holds cross-architecture and correlates with human fMRI data (r = 0.830).

RLHF strengthens internal depth while compressing output expression. The instruct model shows d=0.58 expression-computation dissociation vs d=0.24 for base. Alignment shapes how the model communicates, not how it reasons.
