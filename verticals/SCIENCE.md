# FATHOM for Science

## AI in Research: The Credibility Problem

AI is transforming scientific research, but the community is struggling with verification:
- **Nature (Jan 2023):** Policy requiring disclosure of AI use in papers
- **Science (Jan 2023):** Banned AI as listed author, requires disclosure
- **ICML/NeurIPS:** Require AI use disclosure in submissions
- **NIH/NSF:** Updating grant policies around AI-generated content

The core issue: when a researcher uses AI to generate hypotheses, analyze data, or draft sections of a paper, **nobody can verify whether the AI genuinely reasoned about the science or pattern-matched from its training data.**

A hypothesis generated by deep AI reasoning is potentially valuable. A hypothesis generated by surface recombination of training text is noise dressed up as insight.

## Fathom Depth Certification for Science

### Use Cases

**1. Hypothesis Certification**
When AI generates a scientific hypothesis:
```
Hypothesis: "The correlation between gut microbiome diversity and
cognitive decline may be mediated by short-chain fatty acid
production affecting the blood-brain barrier integrity"

Fathom Score: 81/100 (Grade A)
Depth: 7.64, Entropy: 3.52
Signal: Deep distributed reasoning across mid-layers
Certification: This hypothesis reflects genuine multi-step
reasoning, not surface pattern completion.
```

**2. Peer Review Assistance**
Reviewers can score AI-generated sections of submitted papers. Shallow depth on methodology or analysis sections may indicate low-quality AI assistance.

**3. Literature Review Quality**
AI-generated literature reviews get depth-scored. Reviews showing deep reasoning are synthesizing across sources. Reviews showing shallow depth are concatenating abstracts.

**4. Data Analysis Verification**
When AI interprets experimental results, the depth score indicates whether it engaged in genuine statistical reasoning or surface-level description.
