Subject: Built on your SAE work — reasoning vs recall is structurally visible in the circuits

Hi Neel,

Quick version: we used SAE feature attribution (circuit-tracer, Gemma transcoders from your work) to show that surface recall vs explanatory reasoning produce measurably different circuit topologies. Pre-registered, p=0.000051, n=30 vocabulary-controlled pairs, LOO robust.

The finding: surface recall concentrates activation at output-proximal layers. Reasoning distributes across mid-layers. This holds across Gemma-2-2B and Qwen3-0.6B (depth ratio 1.03 and 1.00 respectively — candidate structural constant).

Two unexpected results:
- Task-specific stochastic resonance: concept-level integration tasks (syllogisms, causal chains) score *deeper* at T=0.3 than T=0.0. Formal-precision tasks (Godel) score shallower. Same pattern as biological stochastic resonance.
- fMRI topology convergence: 0.830 universality coefficient between transformer layer distributions and human prefrontal/hippocampal activation patterns.
